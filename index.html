Word Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

“Word Quest” (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a free word puzzle game made by Sajib. This policy explains what data is stored or processed when you play, and how you can control it.

There are two separate things to understand:

  • (a) Game data (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device by us and is never sent to us.
  • (b) Advertising is handled by a third-party SDK, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), integrated in the Android app. When an ad is requested, data is processed by Google as described in Section 3. We do not control Google’s servers or retention.

We do not operate accounts, we do not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details inside the app, and we do not integrate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob)

The Android app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show a bottom banner and full-screen interstitial ads (on paid Hints and on level completion). The app currently uses Google’s official test ad units, but the SDK is real: whenever an ad is requested, data is processed by Google to deliver the ad, which can include device information and the Android advertising ID supplied by the operating system.

What this means in plain terms:

  • We do not receive, see, or store any advertising data. Your game progress is never sent to Google or to any advertiser.
  • Google processes ad-request data on its own systems under Google’s own privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy and Google’s publisher data information). We cannot describe, limit, or delete what Google retains — only Google can.
  • You can limit ad personalisation and reset the identifier from the device: Android Settings → Google → Ads (opt out of personalised ads / reset advertising ID). Steps vary by Android version.

If production ad units are ever enabled, this policy will be updated before release to describe the live configuration.

4. Sharing and disclosures

  • We do not sell, trade, or share your game data with anyone. It never leaves your device.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • When the AdMob SDK requests an ad, Google processes ad-request data as described in Section 3. That processing is governed by Google’s policies, not by ours, and we receive none of it.
  • We will disclose data only if required by law, and only to the extent the law requires. Such a request could only concern data we actually hold — which, for game data, is nothing, because it stays on your device.

5. Children’s privacy (all ages, including children)

Word Quest is a general word puzzle game intended for all ages, including children. We do not ask children (or anyone) for names, contact details, or any other personal information inside the app, and our code stores only local game progress.

However, because the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK, ad requests are processed by Google as described in Section 3. Parents and guardians should be aware of this, and should know that:

  • Google Play’s Families / target-audience declaration and the app’s content rating for this app have not yet been completed or verified — those store declarations must be finished before any public release, and this policy must not be read as proof of Families-policy compliance.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe ad-related data about your child has been processed by Google in connection with this app, please contact Google under its own policies, and contact us (Section 9) so we can consider what app-side steps are available.

6. Data retention and deletion (what we can and cannot promise)

  • Game data (our side): kept on your device until you delete it. Settings → “Reset progress” deletes all levels, coins, and preferences immediately; uninstalling the app removes it too. Because it never reaches our servers, there is nothing for us to retain afterwards and nothing we could recover.
  • Advertising data (Google’s side): we hold no copy and operate no deletion queue for it. How long Google retains ad-request data, and what deletion or objection rights apply to it, is determined by Google’s own policies — we cannot promise deletion of data we never receive. Resetting or opting out via Android Settings → Google → Ads limits future personalisation but does not delete Google’s past records; see Google’s documentation for what that control does.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will post the new version here and update the “Last updated” date. For significant changes we will make that clear in the app. The current published build uses test ad units; enabling live production ads or adding any consent mechanism would be a significant change disclosed here first.

8. Regional notes (no compliance outcome claimed)

  • EU/EEA/UK: game progress is stored on-device as strictly necessary to provide the game you asked for. Whether any consent mechanism is required for the Google Mobile Ads SDK in your region has not been determined or implemented in this build — no consent banner exists yet, and this policy does not claim GDPR/ePrivacy consent compliance.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your game data. Deleting it is done on-device via “Reset progress” or uninstall. Rights concerning ad-request data held by Google must be directed to Google.

10. Cookies, local storage, and third-party links

Game progress and settings live in the device’s LocalStorage (key wordquest:progress). The app sets no advertising or tracking cookies of its own; any identifiers or storage used for ad delivery belong to Google’s SDK and are governed by Google’s policies, not ours.

Word Quest may contain links to other websites. This policy applies only to Word Quest. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites.
